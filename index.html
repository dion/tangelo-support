Tangelo plays the music files you put on your device, the libraries on your own Subsonic, Navidrome or Jellyfin servers, internet radio stations, podcasts, and your Apple Music library and the Apple Music catalog. It has no accounts, no advertising and no servers of its own.

Get help

Email support@dionyang.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad you are using, which version of iOS, and which version of Tangelo (settings › about shows it). If something went wrong with a particular song, album or server, naming it helps a great deal.

Common questions

A song says “subscription required”. Why?

That line appears on songs that can only play through Apple's own media service, in one of two situations. A song from the Apple Music catalog — one you found by searching or browsing rather than one in your own library — always needs an active Apple Music subscription to play; browsing, searching and reading artist and album pages need no subscription at all. A song from your imported Apple Music library is different: it is assumed playable and only marked once playback has actually been refused. Tracks you bought, and tracks you downloaded to the device, keep playing with no subscription. Tapping a marked row offers you Apple's subscription sheet.

Why does the equalizer only affect some music?

The equalizer sits inside the audio engine Tangelo uses for files stored on the device, so it shapes those and nothing else. Apple Music plays out of process through the system media service, and server streams, radio and podcasts play through a different path; none of them expose the audio for Tangelo to filter. The preset you choose is remembered and applies the moment a local file plays again. The same limit is why Apple Music tracks have no gapless transition.

Why does Last.fm ask me for an API key?

Last.fm requires each application to identify itself with its own API key and secret. Tangelo does not ship one, so scrobbling to Last.fm uses a key you register yourself, free, at Last.fm's API page, and enter in the app. ListenBrainz needs no key — connecting it is a matter of pasting your user token.

What does “Rebuild Library” lose?

It is offered only on the rare launch where the library database cannot be opened at all. Rebuilding finds your songs again from your files, so nothing you own is deleted. What is lost is everything the database held about them: playlists, hearts, quickplay pins, play history and any scrobbles still waiting to be sent. Try Again first — it is the non-destructive option, and it is often enough.

My server does not connect.

Check the URL includes the scheme (https:// or http://) and the port if the server uses a non-standard one, and that any subpath the server is hosted under is included. Test Connection on the server editor reports what the server actually answered. Servers are a Tangelo Pro feature.

My Apple Music library did not import.

Tangelo asks for access to your media library the first time you import, and never before. If it was declined, the import screen says so and offers a route to Settings, where the permission can be granted. Import brings in metadata; whether each song plays depends on the answer above.

What does Tangelo Pro unlock?

One purchase, no subscription: your own servers, the Apple Music catalog, catalog top-ups for picks, Smart DJ and mixview, the equalizer, synced lyrics, and two more games. Catalog playback additionally needs an Apple Music subscription, which is Apple's and not ours to sell — browsing, search and artist pages work without one, and catalog songs will not play. Restore Purchases lives on settings › about and on the unlock screen.
